# Plumb 2.0: open-source log search

**Who.** Plumb is an open-source (Apache-2.0) log search engine written in Rust, maintained by a team of six and about 300 contributors. 14,000 GitHub stars.

**Offer.** Version 2.0: search a billion log lines on one laptop in under a second, with no cluster and no index to babysit. Free to self-host; a hosted plan exists but is not the subject of this launch.

**Audience.** Backend and platform engineers at companies of 10 to 500 people who are tired of paying per gigabyte for log tools.

**Tone.** Precise, confident, a bit wry. Numbers over adjectives. Engineers talking to engineers.

**Avoid.** Purple-to-blue gradients, glowing dark mode, fake terminal windows with blinking cursors, "blazing fast", "revolutionary", rockets.
