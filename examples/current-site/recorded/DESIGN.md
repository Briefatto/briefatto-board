# Sedge Ferry

## Overview

Sedge Ferry runs a twelve-minute crossing between Little Sedge and East Quay. The site combines the calm of a landing sign with the practical order of a timetable. Lead with an immediate direction or promise, then give boarding details where passengers will look for them.

The voice is plainspoken and specific. “Across the cut.” sets the short, full-stop headline pattern. “A twelve-minute crossing for people, bikes and the occasional muddy dog.” gives a useful detail before allowing a small aside. Instructions speak directly to the passenger, as in “Come down to the landing five minutes before departure.” Put departures, payment and boarding instructions in the text of the piece.

## Colours

Paper is the cool pale page ground and becomes text inside an ink panel. Use ink for reading text, the pennant, and the crossing panel. Signal yellow marks the top of that panel, the route label and the filled action. Place ink text on the yellow action. Use the muted rule for dividers in the light sections.

## Typography

Set the wordmark and headings in the browser’s serif face. Use regular weight for headings and bold for the wordmark. The crossing heading steps down on narrow screens. Use the browser’s system interface face for copy, navigation and actions. Reserve monospace for the upper-case route label and departure times. The site uses browser font stacks.

## Parts

**Pennant and wordmark.** A solid, square-ended flag with a triangular notch cut into the right edge sits beside the bold serif name. Keep the pair on one line. It is the masthead signature.

**Crossing panel.** Place a fine yellow band along the top of a dark green rectangle. Inside, stack the large serif title, the upper-case route label, a brief plain-language sentence and one square-cornered yellow action with a directional arrow. Align the stack to the left and keep air around it.

**Service information.** Give the light section a serif heading. Set times in aligned monospace columns. For boarding instructions, put bold labels beside their descriptions. Divide entries with thin green-grey rules. Link back to the companion page from a simple ruled footer.

## Layout

Give the masthead, crossing panel, service section and footer a shared left and right edge. On wide layouts, let the crossing panel keep open space to the right of the copy. On narrow layouts, reduce the headline size and outer padding, let the masthead wrap, and stack boarding labels above their descriptions. Keep the flag, the band and the typographic order at every size.
