# Moller Bread: weekly sourdough subscription

**Who.** Moller Bread is a two-person bakery on the Zwaanshals, Rotterdam, open since 2019. One wood-fired deck oven, long cold ferments, flour from a mill in Groningen.

**Offer.** A weekly sourdough subscription: one loaf a week, picked up at the shop or delivered by bike within Rotterdam-Noord. EUR 9 a week, pause or stop any time. Starts 1 March.

**Audience.** Neighbours aged 25 to 55 who already buy good bread now and then and would like it to be a habit; many cycle, many cook.

**Tone.** Warm, plain, a little dry. Proud of the craft without fuss. Dutch directness in English.

**Avoid.** Rustic clichés (chalkboards, wheat-sheaf ornaments, kraft-paper textures), "artisanal", exclamation marks, stock smiles.
